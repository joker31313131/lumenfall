Lumenfall is a wave-defense arcade game: you are the keeper of the last lighthouse, and the shadows crawl out of the dark to swallow its light. Aim the lamp with your mouse, hold click or Space to burn shadows down, and gather their embers to feed the flame — oil is your ammunition, and it runs dry. Survive all 10 waves, choose one upgrade after each wave cleared, and face the Hollow King on waves 5 and 10. Seven shadow kinds, combo scoring, a shockwave for close calls, and synthesized sound throughout — no assets, no network, no build step.

Keyboard-only play works too: arrows or A/D turn the lamp, Space burns, E fires the shockwave once unlocked, P pauses, M mutes, R restarts.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.
